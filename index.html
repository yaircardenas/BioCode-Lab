Why this and not a video course

🐛
You debug code that does not crash

The dangerous error in science is the one that returns a believable, wrong number. Sixteen challenges train you to see exactly that.

🧩
You can solve it on your phone

The drag-and-drop challenges need no keyboard, and every editor is built for a small screen.

🗝️
Nobody gets stuck forever

After three real attempts and every hint, you can read a verified solution. Getting stuck should not mean quitting.

📡
Nothing to install

Python runs inside your browser. You only need an internet connection; no account is required and your progress stays on your device.
